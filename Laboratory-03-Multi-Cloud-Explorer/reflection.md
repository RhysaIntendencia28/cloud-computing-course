After completing this laboratory activity, I learned a lot about the three major cloud providers: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. 

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is *Google Cloud Platform*. I was impressed by GCP because of its leadership in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. The tools like Vertex AI and BigQuery are very powerful and are used by many big companies for data analysis. I also liked that Kubernetes was created by Google, so their GKE service is the most mature and easy to use.

There are many similarities among AWS, Azure, and GCP. All three of them offer the same core services like Compute, Storage, Networking, and Databases. They all have global infrastructure with multiple regions and availability zones to make sure applications are fast and reliable. They also all provide a web-based management console, free tier for students, and pay-as-you-go pricing. The main difference is their strengths and target customers.

Business requirements play a very important role in cloud selection. For example, a startup with a limited budget should choose AWS because of its large free tier. A company that already uses Microsoft products should choose Azure for easy integration. An AI research company should choose GCP because it has the best AI tools. This means that there is no "best" cloud for everyone. The best cloud depends on what the business needs.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned how to analyze a client's needs and match them to the right platform. I learned that I need to think about cost, existing technology, and future goals before recommending a solution. This activity made me feel like a real IT professional.

This activity improved my GitHub Portfolio because it showed that I can research, compare, and make recommendations about real-world cloud technologies. The folder structure is also more organized now. Overall, this laboratory helped me understand multi-cloud and prepared me for a career in cloud computing.
